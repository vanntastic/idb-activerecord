Multi-Device Sync

A self-contained simulation (no backend) of the reconciliation scenario: seed a phone with thousands of tasks, then sync them down to a laptop that only has a handful of local records. Each device is a separate IndexedDB database; the “cloud” is an in-memory SyncAdapter. Watch the log to see what gets pushed, pulled, and merged.
